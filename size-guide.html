📐 How to Measure Yourself

👕

Chest

Measure around the fullest part of your chest, keeping the tape horizontal under your arms.

🔗

Waist

Measure around the narrowest part of your waistline, usually just above your belly button.

📐

Hip

Measure around the widest part of your hips, keeping the tape parallel to the floor.

💡 Pro Tips for Perfect Fit

🔗 When measuring your waist, breathe normally and don't pull the tape too tight.

👕 For chest, measure at the widest part - usually at the nipples.

📐 For hips, stand with feet together and measure at the fullest part.
